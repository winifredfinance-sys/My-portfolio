A marketplace that connects buyers directly to sellers only earns when checkout completes. Funnel data and user feedback showed where buyers were getting stuck. Fixing it lifted checkout conversion by 18%.

Context

Shopta is a marketplace built to connect buyers directly with sellers and remove the middlemen between them. Sellers list products; buyers find and buy them.

The problem

In a marketplace, checkout is where buyer intent turns into revenue for sellers and for the platform. Funnel insight and user feedback showed friction at that step. [Add: which part of checkout caused the friction and what users said]

Why it mattered

A buyer who reaches checkout has already chosen what to buy. Losing them there wastes everything the marketplace spent to get them that far. Improving checkout lifts revenue without needing more traffic.

My role and team

I led the end-to-end product lifecycle as a contract Product Manager, coordinating a cross-functional team of 12 engineers and designers with Agile practices and bi-weekly releases.

Discovery

I used product analytics to evaluate user journeys through the funnel, and paired the numbers with user feedback so we knew both where buyers dropped out and why.
