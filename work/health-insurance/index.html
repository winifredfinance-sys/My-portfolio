Context

A Nigerian health maintenance organisation (HMO) provides health plans to companies. Client HR teams, and the outsourcing agencies that manage staff for them, need to see who is covered, how the plan is being used and how the numbers reconcile. Members need to see their own plan.

The company and its product names are withheld on purpose. Everything here is described at product level, with no screens or confidential detail.

The problem

For clients, every question about their own people went through the insurer: utilisation, member lists, reconciliation. They waited for answers.

For the insurer, each request was manual work for the team, and onboarding a new agency was slow.

Why it mattered

Every manual request was time the insurer's team spent on look-ups instead of service, and time the client spent waiting. A slow agency onboarding also slows how quickly new client staff get covered and served.

My role and team

I am Product Team Lead for a cross-functional team of 20: three product managers, two senior back-end engineers, two senior front-end engineers, QA and others.

My job is direction and trade-offs: what the team builds, in what order, and what we deliberately leave out. The product managers on my team own their areas day to day; I make sure those areas add up to one product.

Discovery

The pattern was clear in the work itself: clients kept coming back to the insurer for the same kinds of information. [Add: how you confirmed what clients asked for most, such as interviews, request logs or account-manager input]

Key insight

Clients did not need more reports from us. They needed to see their own data without asking.

Options and trade-offs

Bulk upload of dependants: deprioritised. It mattered, and some clients would have liked it. But members can add their own dependants in the enrollee app, so its impact was lower than other work, and time was tight. I chose to ship the core portal well instead of shipping more of it thinly.

Deactivations stay with the insurer. Clients can manage their members in the portal, but deactivations are approved on the insurer's admin side. Self-service where it saves time; control where a mistake would cost a member their cover.

What we built

  • Client portal for HR teams and agencies: view utilisation, view and manage members, see health data summaries and reconciliation data, without contacting the insurer each time. Data syncs from the insurer's admin platform.
  • Enrollee app for members: their plan, benefits, providers and dependants.
