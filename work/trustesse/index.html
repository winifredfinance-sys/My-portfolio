Context

Trustesse works in fintech security, where user trust, fraud exposure and regulation shape what a product can do and how it should do it.

How I approached it

  • Market and competitor research to position the product in the fintech security space and inform product decisions.
  • Stakeholder workshops to align product priorities with customer needs and the company's expansion objectives.
  • A product vision and roadmap for identity verification, with requirements aligned to the relevant regulatory and operational needs.
  • Trust and security features, developed against that roadmap.

The trade-off I manage

Every verification step protects the platform and costs the user time. Too little and fraud gets through. Too much and good users give up. I place friction where the risk is and keep everything else fast.

What I learned

In trust products, alignment is the work. Compliance, operations and the business each see risk differently. Workshops that put those views in one room made the roadmap something everyone would defend.
