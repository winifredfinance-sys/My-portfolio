Context

Edswot is an edtech company working to close the learning gap for children and students, including learning languages from around the world. Learning happens at home, at the learner's own pace, with parents in control.

The problem

Self-paced learning gives learners freedom, and also makes it easy to drift. A course only delivers value, to the learner and to the business, when it is finished. [Add: what learners or instructors told you, if you have it]

My role

I prioritised the product backlog using user feedback, business value and delivery constraints, and delivered the two changes below. I also worked with marketing and delivery teams on go-to-market coordination for new learning modules.

What we built

  • A personalised learning dashboard so each learner could see their own progress and what came next.
  • Automated feedback loops between students and instructors, so instructors heard about a learner's progress without having to chase it.

Outcomes

+22%course completion
+12 ptsNet Promoter Score

Figures from my CV. I don't claim learning-outcome effects beyond these measures.

What I learned

Completion is a relationship metric. The dashboard helped learners see progress, but the feedback loop with instructors is what kept a person on the other side of it.
